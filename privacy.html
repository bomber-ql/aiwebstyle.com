Privacy Policy

How this site handles data, cookies, and advertising.

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Effective immediately

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit www.aiwebstyle.com (the "Site"), why it is collected, and what choices you have. The Site is a free reference library of web design styles; no account is required to use it.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide voluntarily

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever content you include. This is used only to reply to you and is retained for as long as needed to resolve the matter.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and analytics infrastructure logs standard technical data when a page is requested:

  • IP address (in truncated or aggregated form where the provider supports it)
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Referring page and the pages you visit on the Site
  • Date and time of the request
  • Device class (desktop, tablet, mobile) and approximate region

This data is used in aggregate to understand which pages are useful, to diagnose technical problems, and to keep the Site available. We do not attempt to identify individual visitors from it.

2. Cookies

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device. The Site uses cookies in three ways:

PurposeSet byWhat it does
FunctionalityThis SiteRemembers your language choice and the theme you last previewed, so the page does not reset on every visit. Stored in browser local storage.
AdvertisingGoogle and its partnersServes ads and limits how often you see the same ad. May be used to infer interests.
MeasurementGoogleReports aggregate traffic and ad performance.

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking advertising cookies does not prevent you from using the Site; it only affects how ads are personalised.

Site traffic is measured with Vercel Web Analytics, which is cookieless: it records page views, referrers and coarse device or country data in aggregate without setting cookies or storing a persistent identifier for you. Because no cookie is involved, this measurement is not affected by the cookie choices above.

3. Advertising and third-party vendors

The Site is supported by advertising served through Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Google uses the DoubleClick DART cookie in the ads served on this Site. You may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Advertising Policies page.
  • You may opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.com.
  • For more information about how Google uses data from partner sites, see How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are shown a consent message before personalised advertising cookies are set, in line with Google's EU user consent policy. You may change your choice at any time through the consent link in the footer of the page.

4. Third-party services

The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts, is hosted on Vercel (including Vercel Web Analytics for cookieless traffic measurement) and uses Cloudflare for DNS and caching. Each of these providers processes request metadata (including IP address) as part of delivering the content. Links to external sites are provided for reference; once you follow one, that site's own privacy policy applies.

5. Legal bases for processing (EEA/UK)

  • Legitimate interests — serving pages, keeping the Site secure, and measuring aggregate usage.
  • Consent — personalised advertising and any non-essential cookies, which you may withdraw at any time.
  • Contract — responding to a message you send us.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. This Site does not sell personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@aiwebstyle.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Data retention

Aggregate analytics are retained for up to 14 months. Server logs are retained for up to 30 days. Email correspondence is retained for up to 24 months, or longer if needed to resolve an ongoing issue.

8. Children's privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

The Site is served over HTTPS. Because no accounts or payments are involved, we hold very little personal data — which is itself the main safeguard. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will change and the revision will be described here. Continued use of the Site after a change constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@aiwebstyle.com
