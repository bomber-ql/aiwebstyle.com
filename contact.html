What to write about

Corrections

If a preview renders differently from its description, a download link is broken, or a CSS value on a style page is wrong, that is the most useful message you can send. Please include the page URL and what you saw instead.

Style submissions

Suggestions for styles that are missing are welcome, particularly ones that solve a real layout problem rather than adding a novelty effect. A link to a live example is more useful than a description — every style on this site is documented against a working page, not a mood board.

Licensing and reuse

The prompts and CSS snippets on individual pages may be used in your own projects, commercial or not, without attribution. Republishing a page in full, or reproducing the preview pages as your own gallery, needs a written agreement first.

Privacy and data

Requests to access or delete data, questions about cookies, and anything related to advertising on this site are handled under the privacy policy. If you are asking about an advertising identifier, say so in the first line.

What to expect, and what not to

  • A reply within two working days. If a week passes, assume the message was misfiled and send it again.
  • No guest posts, sponsored links, or link exchanges. This site does not sell placement of any kind, and neither the style pages nor the prompt recipes carry paid links.
  • No debugging of your own builds. The prompts are written to be self-contained, but reproducing a specific AI builder's output is outside what one person can support.

Before you write

Include the page URL, your browser and version, and what you expected to see against what appeared. A screenshot helps when the problem is visual. Messages that begin with the URL are answered fastest, because there is nothing to clarify.
