What the Editorial / Magazine style is doing

The single most important number on this page is the measure: the body column is constrained to roughly 62 characters. Everything else — the serif display face, the deck under the headline, the hairline rules instead of cards — supports that. Browsers default to about 140 characters at this width, which is precisely twice what a reader can track without losing the line.

  • Body copy is capped near 62 characters, not the browser default
  • A deck sits between the headline and the body
  • Sections are divided by rules, not by cards
