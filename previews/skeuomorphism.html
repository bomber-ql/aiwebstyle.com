What the Skeuomorphism style is doing

The leather is a stack of repeating linear gradients at slightly different angles, which produces a grain without an image. Stitching is a dashed border inset a few pixels from the edge. Buttons carry three shadows at once: an inset top highlight, an inset bottom shadow, and a small drop shadow — that combination is what makes them look pressable rather than merely raised.

  • Paper grain is repeating gradients, not a texture file
  • Stitching is a dashed border inset from the edge
  • Buttons use inset highlight, inset shadow and a drop shadow together
