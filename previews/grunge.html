What the Grunge Style style is doing

The misprint effect is a hard text-shadow offset by one or two pixels in a contrasting colour, which imitates a second ink pass that did not register. Panels get their wear from layered gradients with irregular stops rather than from a texture image. The palette is restricted to ash grey, bone and one rust tone, because grunge fails the moment it becomes colourful.

  • Type carries a hard offset shadow like a bad second ink pass
  • Wear comes from irregular gradient stops, not images
  • One rust accent against ash and bone
