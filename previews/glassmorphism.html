What the Glassmorphism style is doing

The whole effect rests on one CSS property: backdrop-filter, which blurs whatever sits behind the panel rather than the panel itself. That is why the colour field is drawn as two fixed radial gradients in the body background — the panels have something worth blurring. The hairline border is a 1px rgba white at around 20 percent, and the top edge carries an extra highlight, which is what separates glass from frosted plastic.

  • The panels blur the colour field behind them, not the page content
  • A 1px light border plus a top highlight fakes the edge refraction
  • Small text sits on the darkest part of the field for contrast
