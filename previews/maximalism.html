What the Maximalism / Collage style is doing

Maximalism is not randomness. Each card here is rotated by a fixed, small amount that differs per card, the background is a repeating diagonal stripe, and the type mixes a display serif with a mono label. The composition only holds because the rotations are small and consistent and the palette is limited to four colours — remove either constraint and it reads as a broken layout instead of a busy one.

  • Rotations are fixed per card, not random
  • The diagonal stripes come from a repeating gradient
  • Only four colours, despite the density
