What the Art Deco Style style is doing

Deco is symmetry plus line work. The sunburst behind the headline is two crossed repeating gradients rather than an image, and every frame is a frame within a frame: an outer gold hairline, a 6px gap, then a second line at lower opacity. Letter spacing is applied to capitals only, because tracking lowercase serif text at this width makes it unreadable.

  • The sunburst is two crossed gradients, not a graphic
  • Frames are doubled with a fixed inset between the lines
  • Wide tracking is applied to capitals only
