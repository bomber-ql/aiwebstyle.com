What the Dark Mode style is doing

A dark theme is a contrast problem before it is a colour choice. Surfaces step from #0e1116 to #171c23 rather than jumping to grey, borders sit at roughly 8 percent white so they read as edges without becoming lines, and the accent is reserved for interactive elements only. Nothing is pure black and no text is pure white, because both extremes produce halation on OLED screens at night.

  • Borders are white at 8 percent, visible but never a drawn line
  • The accent appears only on things you can click
  • Surfaces step up in value instead of using shadows
