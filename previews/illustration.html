What the Illustration Focused style is doing

The hand-drawn feel comes from four small decisions rather than from illustration assets: radii that differ per corner, borders that are slightly thicker on one side, a very shallow rotation on blocks, and dividers drawn as short strokes instead of full-width rules. The palette is warm and limited to three tones, which keeps the page from looking like a children's book.

  • Each corner has a different radius, so nothing is machine-round
  • Blocks sit at a fraction of a degree of rotation
  • Dividers are short strokes, not full rules
