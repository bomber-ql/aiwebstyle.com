What the Liquid Glass style is doing

Both this and glassmorphism are blurred panels, so the difference is where the depth sits. Here the top edge is a single 1px inset highlight at 55 percent white — one hard line across the full width — the border is a heavier 28 percent, and there is no drop shadow at all. The card reads as a slab of material rather than a pane floating above the page, and the blur runs at 24px with 170 percent saturation.

  • A single hard inset line along the top edge, not a gradient streak
  • A 28% border and no drop shadow — separation comes from the edge, not from depth
  • Blur at 24px saturate 170%, the strongest in the gallery
