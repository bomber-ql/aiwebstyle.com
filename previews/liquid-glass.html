What the Liquid Glass style is doing

The difference from glassmorphism is the edge. A 1px border made from a gradient — bright at the top, dark at the bottom — imitates the way a thick glass edge bends light, and an inset white shadow at the top adds the illusion of thickness. Blur is capped around 16px; beyond that the panel stops looking like glass and starts looking like fog.

  • The border is a gradient, brighter at the top than the bottom
  • An inset top highlight creates the sense of thickness
  • Blur is capped — more blur reads as fog, not glass
