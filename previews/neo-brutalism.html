What the Neo-Brutalism style is doing

Neo-brutalism keeps the hard 3px border but adds two things brutalism refuses: rounded corners of about 10px and candy-bright fills. The shadow stays at zero blur, offset 8px, so the depth is still printed rather than floating. The tell is the hover state, which translates the element 4px toward the shadow while shrinking the shadow to match — a physical press, not a colour change.

  • Shadow is 8px with zero blur, in solid black
  • Corners are rounded, which is what separates it from brutalism
  • Hover moves the block and shrinks the shadow by the same amount
