What the Acid Graphics style is doing

Acid graphics is the one style here that is allowed to be ugly. The headline is skewed about five degrees, cards are offset by a fixed 6px rather than a random amount, and the palette is lime, magenta and black with nothing in between. The offset has to be systematic: once it becomes random, the page stops reading as a style and starts reading as a rendering bug.

  • The headline is skewed by a fixed angle, not a random one
  • Card offsets are identical everywhere, 6px
  • Three colours only: lime, magenta, black
