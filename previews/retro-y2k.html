What the Retro - Y2K Aesthetic style is doing

The millennial look is built from three tricks: a chrome gradient that runs light-dark-light to imitate metal, a gloss highlight placed at the vertical midpoint of a shape, and iridescent blobs made from soft radial gradients. Type is a wide geometric sans with tight tracking. None of it is skeuomorphic in the leather sense — it imitates software interfaces and consumer electronics, not physical objects.

  • Chrome text is a gradient clipped to the glyphs
  • The gloss is a white-to-transparent band at the midpoint
  • Blobs are soft radial gradients, not images
