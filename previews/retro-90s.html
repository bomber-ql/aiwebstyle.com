What the Retro - 90s Web style is doing

The authenticity comes from HTML-era defaults rather than from a filter: Times New Roman, navy background, yellow text, and beveled borders built from four different border colours on one element. Links are underlined and blue-violet, because that was the only affordance the browser gave you. Centred everything is not a stylistic choice here, it is a reconstruction of what the platform did by default.

  • Bevels are four border colours on a single element
  • Links keep the browser-default underline and visited colour
  • Nearly every block is centre-aligned
