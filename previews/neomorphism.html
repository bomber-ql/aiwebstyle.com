What the Neomorphism style is doing

Elements are the same colour as the background and are shaped only by two shadows: a light one from the top left, a dark one from the bottom right. Reversing that pair, or using inset, presses an element into the surface. The craft is in the shadow values being derived from the background hue rather than being pure white and pure grey, which is what stops the effect looking like a 2010s button.

  • Light and dark shadows are paired and always opposed
  • Pressed elements reverse the pair or use inset
  • The surface and the elements share one colour
