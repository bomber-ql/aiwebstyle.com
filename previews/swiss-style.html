What the Swiss Style / International Typographic Style style is doing

The grid is doing the work, not the typeface. Headlines occupy columns one to nine and the lead paragraph starts at column seven, so the two blocks overlap for three columns. That deliberate asymmetry is what makes a twelve-column grid visible; a symmetrical layout would look the same at eight columns. Red appears twice on the page and nowhere else, which is the entire colour system.

  • The headline and the lead overlap between columns seven and nine
  • Red is used twice on the whole page
  • Sections are separated by 2px rules, not cards
