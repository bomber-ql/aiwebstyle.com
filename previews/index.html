Why the content never changes

Most style galleries show a mood board, a colour palette, or a screenshot of someone else's site. None of those answer the question a builder actually has: what does this style look like when it has to hold a headline, a paragraph, three columns and a call to action all at once?

Holding the content constant removes the variable. When two previews differ, the difference is entirely attributable to the stylesheet — not to different copy lengths, different image choices, or a different amount of whitespace baked into the mockup.

Each demo also carries a short technical note underneath: what the style is actually doing in CSS, three details worth noticing, and the situation where it stops working. The full breakdown, the prompt that generates it, and a downloadable stylesheet live on the matching style page.
