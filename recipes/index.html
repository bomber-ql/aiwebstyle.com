Prompt Recipes

Whole pages, described precisely enough that the first build is the one you keep.

A page is an argument, not a layout

A style gallery tells an AI what a page should look like. It does not tell it what the page has to accomplish, which section answers which objection, or how many items each section may contain. Those three things are what separate a page that reads as designed from one that reads as generated.

Each recipe below fixes the order, exposes the few variables worth changing, and lists the specific ways that page type fails. Copy the assembled prompt, or edit the variables first and copy the version you get.

Why a recipe and not a prompt pack

A prompt pack gives you a hundred sentences and no way to tell which one works. A recipe gives you one page type, the reasoning behind its section order, and the variables that genuinely change the outcome. When a build goes wrong you can point at the section that failed instead of rewriting the whole prompt.

Every recipe here follows the same four-part shape: goal, structure with counts, style block, constraints. The style block is the part most prompts omit, which is why generated pages so often look like a template with a different accent colour.
