What this is, and what it is not

Most galleries show a mood board. That does not answer the only question a builder has.

Most style galleries show a mood board or a screenshot of somebody else's site. Neither answers the question a builder actually has: what does this style look like when it has to hold a headline, three columns, a paragraph and a call to action at the same time — and at 390 pixels wide?

So every entry here is verified against a page that exists. The previews are real HTML rendered with different stylesheets, the motion patterns are working demos rather than videos, and each prompt is written against the CSS that produces the result. Where a style has a known failure — a contrast problem, a layout that collapses at a breakpoint, a technique that drops frames — the page says so.

It is not a template marketplace and it does not sell placement. The prompts and stylesheets are free to use, and the site earns its keep from advertising that never appears on the demo pages themselves.
